Scientific Principles & Theoretical Null Hypothesis

In cricket, the coin toss is modeled as an independent Bernoulli trial with parameter \(p = 0.50\). Short-term fluctuations (streaks of 4 or 5 consecutive tosses) occur regularly by chance. To establish whether any tipster, algorithm, or pattern has predictive validity beyond random luck, we require a minimum sample size \(n \ge 30\), calculate the 95% Wilson Score Confidence Interval, and perform an exact two-sided binomial test against the 50% baseline.

Wilson Score Interval Mechanics

Unlike normal approximation intervals which break down with small samples or near boundaries (0% or 100%), the Wilson Score Interval inverts the score test, guaranteeing coverage probability close to nominal 95%.
